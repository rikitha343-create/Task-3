# Task-3
Data-Visualization
**Insights from Data Visualization (Amazon Dataset)**
After analyzing the dataset using various visualizations (Bar charts, Histogram, Pie chart, Boxplot, Scatter plot, Line plot, and Heatmap), the following insights were observed:
🔹 1️⃣ Category Distribution
A small number of product categories dominate the dataset.
Some categories have significantly higher product counts compared to others.
This indicates higher market demand or larger product variety in specific segments.

🔹 2️⃣ Price Distribution
Most products fall within a mid-price range.
The histogram shows a right-skewed distribution, meaning:
Many products are moderately priced.
Few products are very expensive (outliers).

🔹 3️⃣ Rating Analysis
Majority of products have ratings between 3.5 and 4.5.
Very low-rated products are minimal.
This suggests that most listed products maintain acceptable quality standards.

🔹 4️⃣ Price vs Rating Relationship

Scatter plot shows weak or moderate correlation between price and rating.
Higher price does not necessarily guarantee higher ratings.
Customer satisfaction depends on value, not just price.

**Conclusions**

The Data Visualization task successfully transformed raw data into meaningful insights.
By using Matplotlib and Seaborn in Jupyter Notebook, the dataset was analyzed through various visual representations that enhanced understanding of:
Product category distribution
Price trends and spread
Customer rating patterns
Relationship between price and ratings.
